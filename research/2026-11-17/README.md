# Measurements, 2026-11-17

Last verified: 2026-11-18.

These are the first measurements of the seven vLLM recipes that were estimates until this day: Mellum2.1, K2 Horizon 7B, Qwen3.5-9B, Gemma 4 12B, Gemma 4 E4B, Groot-19B-A3B and DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B, each started with `./llmctl up <id> --runtime vllm` from the shipped recipe.
Six were measured, and DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B failed because vLLM loads its tokenizer with the wrong class.
The same day the llmctl clients e2e ran every shipped recipe on Hermes, Pi and DeepSeek Harness (dsh), and on OpenCode every recipe except vLLM DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B, which had no OpenCode entry then, and the dsh Web UI checklist ran in a browser; their records are the `llmctl-dsh-` folders below, and the curated results are in [docs/clients.md](../../docs/clients.md#verified-on-this-laptop).
The Kimi Code CLI experiments of the same day, behind the Kimi section of docs/clients.md, are in llmctl-kimi-stub/, and the e2e run with Kimi Code as the fifth client, with the lead's strace check and TUI checklist, is in llmctl-kimi-e2e/.
The same day SGLang v0.5.23 ran on this laptop for the first time, started by hand before llmctl had an SGLang adapter: the hands-on run, measurement A of sampling, grammar memory, readiness and cold start, and the design's offline probes are in sglang-runtime/, and their per-model records in the four `sglang-` folders below.
That evening measurement B ran wave 1 at its shipped settings and the wave 2 candidates on SGLang, and checked the vLLM gpt-oss-20b recipe without a network and the vLLM DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B tokenizer fix; its lane report is in sglang-runtime/measure-b/, its per-model records in the `measure-b/` folder of eight `sglang-` folders, and its two vLLM records in `vllm-gpt-oss-20b-offline/` and `vllm-deepseek-r1-0528-qwen3-8b-retry/`.
Late that evening measurement C started the four llama.cpp GGUF recipe drafts through llmctl and checked a pinned-store setting on gpt-oss-20b on SGLang: its lane report is in sglang-runtime/measure-c/, the llama.cpp records in the four `llamacpp-` folders and the gpt-oss-20b record in `sglang-gpt-oss-20b/measure-c/`.
Measurement D, which ran on 2026-11-18 local time, has its lane report in sglang-runtime/measure-d/ and its four per-model records in [research/2026-11-18/](../2026-11-18/README.md).
Measurement E, Qwen3.6-35B-A3B's flushed reading on 2026-11-18, has its lane report in sglang-runtime/measure-e/ and its record in the same date folder.
Measurement C's runs from 21:00 UTC on are dated 2026-11-18 local time, so its wave 3 records and the vLLM DeepSeek-R1-0528-Qwen3-8B cold start are in [research/2026-11-18/](../2026-11-18/README.md).
The curated results are in [vllm/models.md](../../vllm/models.md) (one section per model), [vllm/benchmarking.md](../../vllm/benchmarking.md#results) and the `[evidence]` tables of the recipes in [recipes/vllm/](../../recipes/vllm/).
Do not edit these records by hand.
A script replaced local paths in these records with `$HOME` and `$SCRATCH` before they were copied into the repository.

## Setup

- Machine: MSI Raider 18 Max HX A2WI, RTX 5080 Laptop GPU (16,303 MiB per `nvidia-smi`, 15.47 GiB per CUDA), 61 GiB of RAM, enforced power limit 145 W (140 W in the DeepSeek-R1 record).
- Runtime of the `vllm-` records of the seven recipes: `vllm/vllm-openai:v0.33.0`, started by `./llmctl up` from a pristine export of `main` at 9a2c43a, with `LLMCTL_CLIENTS` empty so that no client file changed, one recipe at a time.
- Start attempts of the seven recipes' `vllm-` records: at most three per recipe, each flag change made in an override copied from `./llmctl show <id> --runtime vllm --as-override` and kept as `override.diff`, with the reason in `attempts.md`.
- Checks of the seven recipes' `vllm-` records: `./llmctl test` (reply, tool call unless chat only, decode speed), the 2026-11-16 probe [llm_probe.py](../2026-11-16/llm_probe.py) (three decodes of up to 1,500 tokens, 8k and 30k prompts sent twice), and one request per thinking level of the recipe.
  The DeepSeek-R1 record used a copy of the probe that records the tool-call step's error and goes on, because the recipe is chat only (`probe-chat-only.diff` in its folder).
- GPU trace of the seven recipes' `vllm-` records: `nvidia-smi` memory, power and temperature every 2 s; the server log of each `vllm-` measurement record stops at `Application startup complete`.
  The e2e's `docker-logs` records keep the request lines after it, with 172.17.0.1, Docker's default `docker0` bridge gateway, which identifies no machine or person.
- SGLang records: the servers of `sglang-runtime/` and the `sglang-<id>/` folders were started by hand with `docker run` on `lmsysorg/sglang:v0.5.23-runtime`, not by `./llmctl up`, with GPU and cgroup memory traced every 1 s, and the power limit read 140 W after the hands-on runs, 145 W before measurement A, 145 W during measurement B and 140 W before both of measurement C's gpt-oss-20b starts ([sglang-runtime/README.md](sglang-runtime/README.md)).
- Measurement C's `llamacpp-` records: `ghcr.io/ggml-org/llama.cpp:server-cuda-b12407`, started by `llmctl up <id> --runtime llamacpp` from a copy of `main` at 5ad8883 with the drafts as an override folder and `LLMCTL_CLIENTS` empty, with GPU and cgroup memory traced every 1 s and `docker stats` every 2 s; the power limit read 140 W before the Gemma 4 12B, Gemma 4 E4B and Mellum2.1 starts and 145 W before the Qwen3.5-9B start, as each record's `.meta` file says.
- Measurement B's vLLM records, `vllm-gpt-oss-20b-offline/` and `vllm-deepseek-r1-0528-qwen3-8b-retry/`: `vllm/vllm-openai:v0.33.0` started by hand with `docker run` as llmctl's `container_spec` builds the shipped recipe, with the changes each record names, GPU and cgroup memory traced every 1 s, and full logs that keep the request lines from Docker's bridge 172.17.0.1.
